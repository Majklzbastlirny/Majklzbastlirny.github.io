    Keyboard & mouse shortcuts

    Board

    • Wheel — zoom   ·   Drag — pan
    • T top side   ·   B bottom   ·   X X-Ray
    • R reset view   ·   Esc deselect
    • G automatic ground plane on / off
    • N net mode — then click any track or pad to light up everything electrically connected to it
    • P part mode — click a part's pads, name it, and it joins the BOM (for boards with no Sprint components)
    • M measure — click two points; snaps to pad centres
    • I board info — stats, drill table, image export
    • Tab (no row focused) — show / hide BOM panel
    • Double-click a part — toggle its "placed" mark

    BOM (when a row is focused)

    • ↑ / ↓ — move selection
    • Tab — next row   ·   Shift+Tab — previous row
    • Enter / Space — toggle "placed"
