How to measure

  1. 1Ø over threads. Close the outside jaws across the thread crests (the peaks). Rotate the part and take the largest steady reading.
  2. 2Pitch. Don't trust one tooth. Line the jaw on a crest, count N crests, read the length, enter both in the span box — pitch = L/(N−1). Vital for fine threads (SMA = 36 TPI).
  3. 3Depth. Optional. Depth-gauge crest→root, or (Ø crest − Ø root)/2. Helps separate 60° / 55° / round forms.
